Additional Information

Shipping & Delivery

We ship nationwide across the United States. Packages are processed and shipped out 5 days a week to get your order to you as quickly as possible.

  • Dispatch Schedule: All orders are packed and shipped within 3 business days of purchase.
  • Coverage: National shipping only.
  • Tracking: Once your package drops with the carrier, you will receive automated tracking details to follow your shipment.
Damaged or Lost Items

If your item arrives damaged or is lost in transit, please contact us immediately with your order details and photos of any damage. We will work directly with you and the shipping carrier to reach a fair resolution.

Refund & Return Policy

Due to the volatile nature of the collectibles market and to prevent tampering or card swapping, all sales of raw singles and sealed products are final. We do not accept returns or exchanges once an item has been shipped.
